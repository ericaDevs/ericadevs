Who I Am

I operate between business and technology.

I am building my career around the intersection of business understanding and technology execution. My Bachelor of Business Information Technology education is giving me a foundation in both organisational thinking and information technology.

This perspective influences how I approach development. I don't want to build software simply because I can write code. I want to understand the problem behind the software and how the solution can create value for its users or organisation.

My practical development journey has taken me from graphic design and UI/UX into web development, programming, databases, backend engineering, business systems, deployment and emerging technologies such as artificial intelligence and automation.

Why BBIT

Learning how business and technology work together.

I chose Bachelor's degree in Business Information Technology because I wanted a course that goes beyond programming and gives me a wider understanding of how organisations work and how technology can support them.

The course has exposed me to areas such as business processes, management, accounting, economics and information systems while also developing my technical skills in programming, databases and software development.

For me, this combination makes BBIT a practical foundation for the kind of work I want to do. It allows me to look at a challenge from the business side, understand what is needed and then use technology to help build a solution.
